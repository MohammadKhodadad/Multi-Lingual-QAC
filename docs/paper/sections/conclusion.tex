We presented a benchmark-construction pipeline for multilingual legal retrieval from JRC-Acquis. The pipeline treats \texttt{CELEX} as a stable cross-lingual document anchor, separates retrieval and generation text views, filters source documents through configurable legal-substance heuristics, generates same-language legal QA supervision, verifies cross-language answer support before retaining translated positives, and packages the result in standard Hugging Face and MTEB retrieval form.

The current draft still leaves the empirical results table open until the final dataset run and benchmark sweep are frozen. Once those numbers are finalized, the remaining paper work is to insert the model comparison results, summarize language- and variant-specific behavior, and connect the quantitative findings back to the construction audit report and the known limitations of document-level legal relevance.
