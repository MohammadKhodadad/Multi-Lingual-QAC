## Why a fresh data layer (read this)

The existing `common.core_per_query()` reads the cached HF `qrels` config, which is **stale at the old
137-query release**. The current run has **524 queries / 1284 qrels** (`run_metadata.json`). So gold was
rebuilt: `gold(query) =` every language version of the query's source patent that exists in the current
`multilingual_GP` corpus, keyed by `publication_number`. This reproduces the authoritative per-query gold
count `n_relevant` (from `question_analysis/question_level_metrics.csv`) for **all 524 queries** (1284
pairs total), and the per-model Recall@10 matches the official MTEB scores exactly (embeddinggemma 0.5740,
bge-m3 0.5091, …). `mode ∈ {technical, semantic}` is taken from `question_level_metrics.csv`.

**`gte-base` is dropped from every figure** (degenerate encoder, corpus-wide Recall@10 ≈ 0.005). All plots
average over the remaining **8 models**.

**cross vs same** (per the definition requested): a query whose gold lives in ≥2 languages is
**same-language** if at least one gold doc is in the query language, otherwise **cross-language** (all gold
foreign). Result: 261 same / 263 cross queries; 20% of all 1284 query→gold pairs are same-language, **80%
are cross-language**.

## Metric definitions (computed on cross/same gold subsets)

- **XRC** (Cross-lingual Reading-Cost multiplier) `= mean D(cross) / mean D(same)`, where `D` is the
  per-query first-relevant reading depth (rank of the first relevant doc). Never-found twins have an
  infinite rank, so they are **capped at MAXRANK=1000** before averaging — i.e. XRC is a **lower bound**
  under censoring (`XRC_mean_censored=True`; see `cross_censored_frac` / `same_censored_frac`). `cross`
  domain = 524 cross-gold queries; `same` domain = 261 same-gold queries. (`xrc_per_model.csv` also reports
  the mean depths `Dmean_cross` / `Dmean_same`.)
- **RRC@K** (Re-ranker Recoverability Ceiling) `= mean(first_cross_rank ≤ K)` — fraction of cross-gold
  twins inside top-K. `L_inf = 1 − RRC@1000` is the structural floor no top-1000 re-ranker can recover.
- **ARI@K** (Alignment-Recoverability Index) `= L_inf / (1 − RRC@K)` — the alignment-only share of the
  residual after a cheap top-K re-ranker. Higher ⇒ what's left is an alignment problem, not a depth one.

## Headline numbers (524 queries, 8 models)

- Query-language difficulty: en 0.47 > fr 0.43 > es ≈ zh 0.38 > de 0.35 (Recall@10).
- Question type × direction: technical/cross 0.24, technical/same 0.34, semantic/cross 0.48,
  semantic/same 0.55. Technical questions are the trap; same > cross everywhere.
- Pair structure: 80% of query→gold pairs are cross-language; de/es/zh queries are 90–95% cross.
- RRC ceiling (top-10 / top-100): embeddinggemma recovers 60% of cross-gold twins with a top-10 re-ranker,
  78% with top-100, leaving 22% never in top-100; over the full list RRC@1000 0.93 (7% structural floor).
  ARI@100 ranges 0.32 (embeddinggemma) → 0.48 (SapBERT).
- XRC (mean reading depth, censored ranks capped at 1000): embeddinggemma ~1.7× (mean same 79 → cross 136),
  e5-large-instruct ~6.9× (mean same 69 → cross 477; great monolingual, catastrophic cross-lingual).
- Own vs foreign gold: own-language Recall@10 is far higher for every model; gap ranges from +0.12
  (granite) to +0.60 (e5-large-instruct), all *** significant.
